Your first spa facial: what to expect

2 November 2026 · Royal Glow

A facial is one of the most relaxing treatments you can book, and it is a good place to start if you are new to the spa.

Before you arrive

  • Arrive about ten minutes early so you can settle in.
  • Come with clean skin if you can, but don't worry if you are wearing make-up: we remove it.
  • Tell your therapist about any allergies, sensitive skin or products you are using.

During the treatment

Your therapist cleanses, gently exfoliates, applies a mask suited to your skin and finishes with a face and shoulder massage. You can talk or simply rest.

Afterwards

Drink some water, avoid heavy make-up for the rest of the day, and use sunscreen.
