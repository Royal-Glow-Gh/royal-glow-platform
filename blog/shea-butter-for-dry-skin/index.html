Why shea butter works so well for dry skin

2 November 2026 · Royal Glow

Shea butter comes from the nuts of the shea tree, which grows across the savannah of northern Ghana and the wider region.

What makes it special

  • It is rich and slow to absorb, so it forms a soft layer that helps skin hold on to moisture.
  • Unrefined shea keeps more of its natural character: a nutty smell and an ivory colour.
  • It melts at body temperature, so it spreads easily once warmed in your hands.

How to get the most from it

Apply it straight after a bath or shower, while your skin is still slightly damp. Focus on the driest areas: elbows, knees, heels and hands.

If your skin is oily or prone to breakouts, use it on your body rather than your face.
