Headline and introduction

On the Classic and Showcase styles, the first slide's own headline and text are used instead, if it has them.

Promise badges

Short promises shown as a line (Editorial) or a gold strip (Showcase). Up to 4; leave blank to hide.

Three highlights

Cards (Classic), numbered rows with photos (Editorial) or photo tiles (Showcase). Showcase also uses the third as the stockist band.

Section photos

Hero slides

The big banner at the top of the Classic and Showcase home pages. With one slide it's a still banner; with two or three, they change every 7 seconds (paused while someone hovers or touches, and never moving for visitors who switch off animations). Up to 3 active slides are shown, in order. Blank headline or text means the tagline and introduction are used.

New announcement

A slim gold bar above the header on every page. Up to 3 current messages rotate. It loads live, so changes show at once, no publishing needed. Dates are optional.

Announcements

Only real words from real customers, published only when the customer has agreed. Invented or reworded reviews mislead customers. Light edits for spelling are fine; changing the meaning is not.

Testimonials

Customer replies from feedback links arrive here as drafts. Published ones appear on the home page, the About page and, if linked to a treatment, in spa booking.

Ask a customer "How was your visit?"

Creates a one-time link to a short form on the website. Send it after a completed spa visit or a delivered order. The reply comes in as a draft testimonial for review. Links work for 60 days.
