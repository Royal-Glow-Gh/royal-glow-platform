Royal Glow: Supabase account email templates
Supabase -> Authentication -> Emails -> Templates. For each template: paste the Subject, then replace the Message body (Source / HTML) with the file's contents, Save.

confirm-signup.html  ->  Subject: Confirm your email for Royal Glow
reset-password.html  ->  Subject: Reset your Royal Glow password
change-email.html  ->  Subject: Confirm your new email address
magic-link.html  ->  Subject: Your Royal Glow sign-in link
invite-user.html  ->  Subject: You're invited to Royal Glow

Reauthentication (if shown) can stay as Supabase's default: it only sends a code.
