Products on the website

Only products and resale goods with a retail price (set it in Sales → Price list) can be shown. Photos: square JPG or PNG, under 2 MB.

Payment and contact

Customers paying by MoMo see the number and name, and use their order number as the reference.

Delivery areas

Customers choose an area at checkout and see its fee. Paid to rider: the customer pays the rider directly, so the fee is not added to what they pay Royal Glow. Customers outside these areas can choose Somewhere else; you then set the fee on the order before confirming it.
