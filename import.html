Books start date

The first day of the records in this system. Opening balances (stock, what customers owe, what is owed to suppliers, cash and bank) are as at this day; purchases, sales and payments are imported from this day on. If you will import older records, choose the date those records start from.

Suggested order: 1 Products and prices · 2 Customers · 3 Suppliers · 4 Opening stock · then cash, bank and other balances in Accounts → Import (opening balances).
