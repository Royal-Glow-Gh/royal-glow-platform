\section*{The job in one paragraph}
Royal Glow makes skincare products and runs a spa in Ghana. The new website (\url{https://royalglowgh.com}) and online shop (\url{https://shop.royalglowgh.com}) are built and working; they now need real artwork. Please supply the files in the list in section~\ref{sec:list}, each at the exact size given. You do \textbf{not} need to design web pages, write code or put text on the images: the website lays out the pages and types the headlines over your photos. Your job is well-composed, retouched photographs cropped to the right shapes. The logo and icons already exist and are \textbf{not} being redesigned.

You can see every place an image goes, with dashed gold boxes showing the name and size of each missing picture, at \url{https://royalglowgh.com/preview/}. Look at it on a computer and on a phone before you start.

\section{Rules for every file}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Exact pixel size} as listed. Larger is fine if the shape (aspect ratio) is the same; smaller will look soft and the upload page will warn about it.
  \item \textbf{Photos:} JPEG (quality about 80) or WebP, \textbf{sRGB} colour (not CMYK, not Adobe RGB). Each file \textbf{under 500\,KB}; slides under 700\,KB. Files over 5\,MB are refused by the upload page.
  \item \textbf{No text, prices or buttons inside photos.} Headlines and buttons are typed on the website, so they stay sharp, can be changed and can be read by Google. The only text allowed is what is really in the photo, such as a product label.
  \item \textbf{Keep the subject in the safe area} (section~\ref{sec:safe}). The website crops pictures for different screens and puts text over parts of them.
  \item \textbf{File names:} lower case, hyphens, no spaces, exactly as in the ``File name'' column. For products and blog covers, use the product or article name, e.g.\ \key{product-shea-body-butter.jpg}.
  \item \textbf{Real products only.} Show Royal Glow's own products and spa. No competitor products or logos in view.
  \item \textbf{Rights.} Any stock photo must be licensed for commercial web use (keep the licence). Anyone recognisable in a photo must have signed a model release giving Royal Glow permission. Never use images found on Google or social media.
  \item \textbf{Hand over the source files too} (PSD, AI, Figma or RAW), so the images can be re-cropped later.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Photo mood.} Warm, natural light; gold, cream, honey and deep brown tones; clean surfaces (wood, linen, stone); real hands and skin, lightly retouched (no heavy skin smoothing). Shea butter, oils and natural ingredients make good supporting props. Leave calm, uncluttered space where the safe-area drawings show text.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Slides, computer:} the headline covers the left 45\%; one style also darkens the bottom third. Very wide screens trim about 10\% top and bottom.
  \item \textbf{Slides, phone:} text covers the lower half; phones may trim up to 15\% from each side.
  \item \textbf{Editorial hero:} the arch cuts the top corners; keep the subject in the central 70\% of the width.
  \item \textbf{Making, spa, stockists:} in one style the title covers the bottom 40\%. The spa photo may also be cropped taller, so keep its subject centred.
  \item \textbf{Shop banner:} keep the subject in the upper right; the headline sits on the left.
  \item \textbf{About banner:} shown as a wide strip 220--420\,px tall, so the top and bottom are often cut off.
\end{itemize}

\section{Handing over}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Put the final files in one shared folder (Google Drive or similar) with three sub-folders: \key{brand}, \key{website}, \key{products-blog}. Put source files in a fourth, \key{source}.
  \item For the first review, send the slide, home and about images as low-resolution drafts. Royal Glow uploads them to the Website page and checks them in the preview on a computer and a phone, then sends back comments.
  \item After approval, send the finals. Royal Glow staff upload them on the staff Website, Online shop and Blog pages (a new share image, if made, is added by Royal Glow's developer). You do not need any login.
  \item Include a short note listing any stock photos used, with their licence links, and confirming model releases for anyone recognisable.
\end{enumerate}
